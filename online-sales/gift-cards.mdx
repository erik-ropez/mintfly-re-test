Gift cards are one of the most effective ways to drive new guest visits and increase upfront revenue. With Rocket Effect, you can sell branded digital gift cards directly from your website, let guests schedule delivery for a future date, and track the full lifecycle of every card — from purchase through redemption — without any manual effort.

## How Gift Cards Work

When a guest purchases a gift card through your Rocket Effect-powered storefront, they choose a value, personalize the card with an occasion and optional message, and send it to a recipient by email. The recipient receives a digital card with a unique code they can redeem at your venue or apply during online checkout. Physical gift card programs can also be managed through Rocket Effect if your point-of-sale system supports integration.

## Set Up Gift Card Sales

<Steps>
  <Step title="Enable the Gift Card Module">
    Navigate to **Online Sales → Gift Cards** and toggle **Enable Gift Card Sales** to on. This activates the gift card storefront and generates an embeddable widget you can place on your website. Copy the embed code from the **Widget Code** tab and add it to your site's gift card page.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Configure Card Values">
    Go to **Online Sales → Gift Cards → Values** and define the amounts guests can choose from. You can create fixed denominations (for example, $25, $50, $100) and optionally allow a custom amount within a range you specify. Toggle **Allow Custom Amount** and set a minimum and maximum to give guests flexibility while protecting your margins.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Add Occasion Types and Visuals">
    Under **Online Sales → Gift Cards → Customization**, add the occasion types guests can select at checkout — for example, Birthday, Holiday, Just Because, or Congratulations. Upload a card image or design for each occasion so the gift card feels personal and on-brand. Supported image formats are PNG and JPG at a minimum of 600 × 400 pixels.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Enable Scheduled Delivery">
    Toggle **Allow Scheduled Delivery** to let purchasers choose a future delivery date for the gift card email. This is especially valuable for birthday and holiday purchases made days or weeks in advance. Cards are queued and sent automatically at the selected date and time in the recipient's time zone.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Connect Your Gift Card System (Optional)">
    If you use a point-of-sale or card system that supports gift card integration, go to **Settings → Integrations** and connect the relevant provider. Once connected, gift card balances sync between Rocket Effect and your POS, allowing staff to accept redemptions at the register without manual balance lookups.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Publish and Test">
    Publish your widget page and purchase a test gift card using a small value. Confirm the delivery email arrives, the card code is valid, and the balance appears correctly in your dashboard under **Online Sales → Gift Cards → Active Cards**. Once verified, promote the gift card store across your marketing channels.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Gift Card Configuration Options

The table below summarizes the key settings available when configuring your gift card program.

| Setting | Description | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| **Fixed Denominations** | Preset dollar amounts guests can select | Add as many as needed; recommended to offer 3–5 options |
| **Custom Amount** | Guest-entered value within a min/max range | Set a minimum of at least $5 to avoid micro-transactions |
| **Occasion Types** | Labels guests choose to personalize the card | Examples: Birthday, Holiday, Thank You, Just Because |
| **Card Visuals** | Images displayed on the digital gift card | Upload one image per occasion; PNG or JPG, min 600 × 400 px |
| **Personalized Message** | Optional text field for the purchaser | Displayed in the recipient's delivery email |
| **Scheduled Delivery** | Future delivery date and time | Sent automatically in the recipient's time zone |
| **Expiry Date** | Optional expiration on gift card value | Check local regulations before enabling; leave blank for no expiry |
| **Single-Use Codes** | One redemption per card code | Recommended for digital gift cards to prevent misuse |

## Track Gift Card Activity

All gift card activity is visible in **Online Sales → Gift Cards → Activity**. Use the filters to view cards by status:

- **Sent** — purchased and delivered to the recipient
- **Redeemed** — applied at checkout or in-venue
- **Partially Redeemed** — balance partially used with remaining value
- **Unredeemed** — purchased but not yet used (potential follow-up opportunity)
- **Expired** — past expiry date if expiration is enabled

Export any view to CSV for reconciliation or to identify unredeemed cards you can target with a re-engagement campaign.

## Use Gift Cards in Promotions and Campaigns

Gift cards integrate directly with Rocket Effect's marketing tools. Add a gift card call-to-action button to your email and SMS campaigns, embed the gift card widget in a popup or landing page, and feature gift cards in your seasonal promotions. You can also offer bonus credit on gift card purchases (for example, buy a $50 gift card and receive $10 in bonus play) by combining a gift card sale with a discount promotion.
