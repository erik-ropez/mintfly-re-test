Integrations are the foundation of everything Rocket Effect does. By connecting your existing cashless card systems and point-of-sale platforms, you give Rocket Effect access to real-time guest data — card activity, visit frequency, spending history, and gameplay records — that powers your guest profiles, campaign targeting, and analytics. Without integrations, you're working with manual data; with them, your marketing runs on live intelligence.

## What Data Flows Into Rocket Effect

When you connect an integration, Rocket Effect continuously syncs the following data from your systems:

| Data Type | Description |
|---|---|
| **Guest Records** | Name, email, phone, birthday, and registration date for each customer in your system |
| **Card Balances** | Current balance on each guest's active play card |
| **Game Play History** | Session-level gameplay data including games played, frequency, and favorite attractions |
| **Transaction History** | Itemized purchase records, reload amounts, and spending totals per guest |

This data populates guest profiles automatically and updates in real time as guests visit and play. Your campaign audiences and analytics always reflect the most current activity from your floor.

## Setting Up an Integration

Connecting a new integration takes only a few minutes from your Rocket Effect dashboard.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the Integrations section">
    Log in to your Rocket Effect account at [rocketeffect.app](https://rocketeffect.app) and navigate to **Integrations** in the main sidebar.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select your platform">
    Find your cashless card system or POS platform in the list of available integrations and click **Connect**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter your credentials">
    Provide the API key, location ID, or login credentials required by your platform. Each integration's setup guide includes the exact credentials needed and where to find them in your existing system.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Authorize the connection">
    Confirm the connection and allow Rocket Effect to begin syncing data. Initial sync time varies by the size of your guest database — most locations complete the first sync within a few minutes.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Verify the sync">
    Navigate to **Guests** and confirm that guest records from your connected system are appearing. Check a known guest's profile to verify that card balance and visit history have populated correctly.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Multi-Location Support

If you operate more than one FEC location, you can connect each location's systems under a single Rocket Effect account. Each integration connection is associated with a specific location, allowing you to:

- Segment campaign audiences by location
- View analytics per location or rolled up across all venues
- Manage all guest profiles from one unified dashboard

To add a second location, navigate to **Account Settings → Locations** and follow the prompts to register the new venue before connecting its integrations.

<Note>
  If your system isn't listed above, contact Rocket Effect support. The platform supports additional integrations on a case-by-case basis, and the team can advise on custom connection options for your specific setup.
</Note>
