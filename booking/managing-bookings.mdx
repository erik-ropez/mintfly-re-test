Once guests start booking through your website, all reservations flow into a central dashboard where you can track upcoming events, make changes, communicate with guests, and export records for reporting. Whether a guest needs to move their party to a different date or you need to free up a room for a private event, every action takes just a few clicks.

## Viewing Your Bookings

Navigate to **Booking → Bookings** to see all your reservations. You can switch between two views depending on what you need:

- **Calendar view** — displays bookings visually by day, week, or month so you can spot availability at a glance
- **List view** — shows bookings in a sortable table with columns for guest name, date, package, guest count, payment status, and waiver status

Use the filter bar at the top of either view to narrow results by **date range**, **booking status** (confirmed, pending, cancelled), or **package type**.

## Editing, Cancelling, and Rescheduling

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="Edit a Booking">
    Use the Edit flow to update any details of a confirmed reservation — such as guest count, contact information, or package selection — without cancelling and rebooking.

    <Steps>
      <Step title="Open the Booking Record">
        From **Booking → Bookings**, locate the reservation using the calendar or list view. Click the booking to open the detail panel.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Click Edit">
        Select **Edit Booking** in the top-right corner of the detail panel.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Update the Details">
        Modify any of the following fields as needed:

        - **Date and time** — select a new slot from available openings on the calendar
        - **Package** — switch to a different tier or bundle
        - **Guest count** — adjust the headcount (the system will warn you if the new count exceeds room capacity)
        - **Contact information** — update the host's name, email address, or phone number
        - **Add-ons** — add or remove optional extras
      </Step>
      <Step title="Save and Notify">
        Click **Save Changes**. If you changed the date, time, or package, toggle **Send Updated Confirmation to Guest** before saving so the guest receives an email with the revised details automatically.
      </Step>
    </Steps>
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Cancel a Booking">
    <Steps>
      <Step title="Open the Booking Record">
        From **Booking → Bookings**, locate the reservation and click it to open the detail panel.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Initiate Cancellation">
        Click **Cancel Booking**. A confirmation dialog will appear asking you to confirm the action and choose how to handle payment.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Choose Refund or Credit">
        Select one of the following options for any payments already collected:

        - **Full refund** — returns the full amount to the guest's original payment method (typically within 5–10 business days)
        - **Partial refund** — specify a custom amount to return, keeping the remainder as a cancellation fee
        - **Account credit** — convert the payment to a credit balance the guest can apply to a future booking
        - **No refund** — retain the full payment per your cancellation policy
      </Step>
      <Step title="Notify the Guest">
        Toggle **Send Cancellation Email to Guest** to automatically send a message confirming the cancellation and communicating any refund or credit details. Click **Confirm Cancellation** to complete the process.
      </Step>
    </Steps>
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Reschedule a Booking">
    <Steps>
      <Step title="Open the Booking Record">
        From **Booking → Bookings**, locate the reservation and click it to open the detail panel.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Click Reschedule">
        Select **Reschedule** in the detail panel. This opens a date-and-time picker filtered to your available openings.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Select a New Date and Time">
        Choose the new date and time slot. The calendar will only display slots where the required room and staff capacity are available, preventing accidental double-booking.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Confirm and Notify">
        Click **Confirm Reschedule**. Rocket Effect automatically sends the guest an updated confirmation email with the new date, time, and all original booking details. The original date is released back to your available calendar immediately.
      </Step>
    </Steps>
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

<Tip>
  Use the pre-event reminder message (sent 48–72 hours before the booking) to mention popular add-ons the guest did not select at checkout — extra play credits, premium food packages, or themed decor upgrades. A well-timed reminder is one of the easiest ways to increase per-party revenue without any additional marketing spend.
</Tip>

## Communicating with Guests

Beyond automated confirmations and reminders, you can send custom messages to any guest directly from their booking record. Open the reservation, scroll to the **Messages** section, and click **Send Message**. Choose whether to send via **email**, **SMS**, or both, then type your message. All sent messages are logged in the booking record for reference.

Common uses for custom messages include:

- Sharing parking or check-in instructions before a large group event
- Informing guests of a room change or schedule update
- Following up after the event to request a review or referral

## Exporting Booking Data

To download a report of your reservations, go to **Booking → Bookings**, apply any filters you want (date range, status, package), and click **Export**. Choose **CSV** or **Excel** format. The export includes all visible columns — guest name, booking date, package, guest count, total revenue, payment status, and waiver status — making it straightforward to import into your own reporting tools or share with your accountant.
