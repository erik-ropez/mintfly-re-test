Rocket Effect makes it easy for guests to reserve birthday parties and group events right from your website, without a single phone call. You define the packages, set pricing and capacity, and the platform handles everything from deposit collection to confirmation emails — so your staff can focus on delivering a great experience instead of chasing paperwork.

## How It Works

Once you connect your booking platform and configure your party packages, a booking widget becomes available for your website. Guests choose a package, pick a date and time, customize with add-ons, pay a deposit or full amount, and receive an automated confirmation — all in one seamless flow.

## Setting Up Party Booking

<Steps>
  <Step title="Connect Your Booking Platform">
    In your Rocket Effect dashboard, navigate to **Settings → Integrations** and select your preferred booking platform. Rocket Effect supports direct connections to Clubspeed, RTS, and other FEC-focused systems. Enter your API credentials and click **Save & Test** to confirm the connection.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Configure Your Party Packages">
    Go to **Booking → Party Packages** and click **New Package**. Give each package a clear name (e.g., "Ultimate Birthday Blast") and fill in the details:

    - **Included items** — room, food, activities, and play credits
    - **Price** — flat rate or per-person pricing
    - **Capacity** — minimum and maximum guest counts
    - **Duration** — total event length including setup and cleanup time

    Click **Save** when your package details are complete.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add Custom Combos and Add-Ons">
    On the **Add-Ons** tab within each package, click **Add Item** to create optional upgrades guests can select at checkout. Common add-ons include:

    - Extra food or beverage options (pizza upgrades, juice boxes)
    - Additional play credits or game tokens
    - Party favors and goodie bags
    - Themed decorations

    Set a price and description for each add-on so guests can make informed choices before they arrive.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Configure Payment Collection">
    Under **Booking → Payment Settings**, choose whether to collect a **deposit** or **full payment** at the time of booking. Enter your deposit amount (flat dollar value or percentage of total). Rocket Effect accepts all major credit and debit cards through its integrated payment processor.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Publish Your Booking Widget">
    Go to **Booking → Website Widget** and copy the embed code. Paste it into your website's party page. Test the flow by submitting a test booking to confirm packages, pricing, and confirmation emails are all working correctly.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
  Require a deposit at booking rather than collecting full payment on arrival. Even a modest deposit — 20–30% of the total — significantly reduces no-shows and last-minute cancellations, protecting your venue's revenue and calendar.
</Tip>

## Booking Confirmation Emails

When a guest completes a reservation, Rocket Effect automatically sends a confirmation email containing the booking date and time, package details, total amount paid, and any remaining balance due. You can customize the confirmation email template under **Settings → Email Templates → Party Booking Confirmation**.

## Managing Availability

Keep your calendar accurate so guests only see times you can actually accommodate.

- **Block dates** — Go to **Booking → Calendar**, select a date, and click **Block** to mark it unavailable (e.g., private events or maintenance days).
- **Set room capacity** — Under **Settings → Venues & Rooms**, assign each bookable space a maximum guest count. The system will automatically prevent overbooking.
- **Adjust availability windows** — Define which days of the week and which time slots are open for party bookings under **Booking → Availability Schedule**.

<Note>
  Changes to your availability schedule take effect immediately. If you need to close a date that already has bookings, you will need to manually contact those guests and reschedule before blocking the date.
</Note>
