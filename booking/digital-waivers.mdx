Digital waivers let every guest in your party sign a liability agreement online before they walk through your door. When a booking is confirmed, Rocket Effect automatically sends a waiver link to the party host, who can forward it to their group. By the time guests arrive, your staff can check everyone in with a single status confirmation instead of managing a stack of paper forms at the front desk.

## What Digital Waivers Include

Each waiver you create in Rocket Effect captures the information you need to protect your business and comply with your policies:

- **Guest name and date of birth**
- **Signature** (drawn or typed)
- **Guardian name and signature** for any minor guests
- **Date signed**
- Any **custom fields** you add (emergency contact, health disclosures, photo consent, etc.)

Completed waivers are stored in your dashboard and linked directly to the corresponding booking record.

## Creating and Activating a Waiver

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the Waiver Builder">
    In your Rocket Effect dashboard, navigate to **Booking → Waivers** and click **New Waiver**. Give your waiver a name that reflects its purpose, such as "General Participation Waiver" or "Birthday Party Liability Release."
  </Step>
  <Step title="Write or Paste Your Waiver Text">
    Enter the full text of your waiver in the document editor. You can type directly, paste from an existing document, or use a starter template. Format the text with headings and paragraphs as needed for readability.

    <Note>
      Consult a licensed attorney in your jurisdiction before publishing your waiver. Legal requirements for liability waivers — including enforceability, minor consent rules, and required disclosures — vary by location. Rocket Effect provides the delivery mechanism; your legal counsel should approve the document content.
    </Note>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Configure Required Fields">
    On the **Fields** tab, enable the data fields you want every signer to complete:

    - **Full name** (required by default)
    - **Date of birth** — useful for age verification
    - **Signature** — drawn on screen or typed
    - **Guardian name and signature** — triggers automatically when a guest's age indicates they are a minor
    - **Custom fields** — add checkboxes, text inputs, or dropdown menus for any additional information you need

    Mark each field as **Required** or **Optional** using the toggle next to its name.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Assign the Waiver to Party Bookings">
    Go to the **Assignment** tab and select which booking types should trigger this waiver. You can assign one waiver to all party bookings or create separate waivers for different event types (e.g., school groups vs. birthday parties).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Activate the Waiver">
    Click **Save & Activate**. The waiver is now live. Going forward, every confirmed booking that matches your assignment rules will automatically trigger a waiver link sent to the party host's email address.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Sending Waiver Links

You do not need to manually send waiver links. As soon as a booking is confirmed, Rocket Effect emails the party host a personalized link and instructs them to share it with their guests before the event. Each guest who opens the link signs their own waiver, and their response is recorded individually in your dashboard.

If you need to resend the link manually — for example, if a host misplaces the email — go to **Booking → Bookings**, open the relevant reservation, and click **Resend Waiver Link**.

## Tracking Waiver Completion

From **Booking → Bookings**, open any reservation and select the **Waivers** tab. You will see a live list of all expected guests alongside their waiver status:

| Status | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Signed** | Guest has completed and submitted the waiver |
| **Pending** | Waiver link was sent but not yet completed |
| **Not Sent** | Guest was added after the initial send; resend manually |

You can download a PDF copy of any individual signed waiver directly from this view.

## Automated Waiver Reminders

Rocket Effect sends automated reminder messages to guests who have not yet signed as the event date approaches. By default, reminders go out **48 hours** and **24 hours** before the booking. To adjust the timing or message content, go to **Settings → Automation → Waiver Reminders**.

<Tip>
  Encourage party hosts to share the waiver link with their group as soon as they receive the confirmation email. The more guests who sign in advance, the faster your check-in process on event day.
</Tip>

## Check-In Flow on Arrival Day

When guests arrive, your staff can pull up the booking from **Booking → Bookings** on any device with a browser. The **Waivers** tab shows at a glance who has signed. For guests who have not yet signed, you can open the waiver link on a tablet and have them sign on-site before they enter the play area.
