The Rocket Effect analytics dashboard gives you an at-a-glance view of everything that matters for your Family Entertainment Center. From online card sales and campaign results to upcoming bookings and your most loyal guests, all key performance data is consolidated in one place so you can make faster, more confident decisions.

## What the Dashboard Shows

When you open the dashboard, Rocket Effect displays a set of widgets pulled from your connected integrations and platform activity. Each widget refreshes automatically and reflects the date range and location you have selected.

| Widget | What It Displays |
|---|---|
| **Revenue Overview** | Online play card sales, gift card sales, and booking revenue totals for the selected period |
| **Campaign Summary** | Number of active campaigns, total message sends, average open rate, and recent conversions |
| **Top Guests Leaderboard** | Your highest-spending and most-frequent visitors, ranked by total spend or visit count |
| **Recent Bookings** | Upcoming party and event bookings with date, party type, and guest name |
| **Leads Captured** | New email and phone subscribers acquired through popups, landing pages, and campaigns |

## Filtering Your Dashboard

### By Date Range

To focus on a specific period, use the date picker at the top of the dashboard.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the date picker">
    Click the date range selector in the top-right corner of the dashboard.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose a preset or custom range">
    Select a preset such as **Last 7 Days**, **Last 30 Days**, or **This Month**, or enter a custom start and end date.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Apply the filter">
    Click **Apply**. All dashboard widgets update immediately to reflect the selected period.
  </Step>
</Steps>

### By Location

If you manage multiple FEC locations under one Rocket Effect account, you can filter the dashboard to a single location or view aggregate data across all locations.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the location selector">
    Click the **Location** dropdown near the top of the dashboard.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select a location">
    Choose an individual location from the list, or select **All Locations** to see combined data.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review updated widgets">
    Each widget re-renders to reflect only the data tied to your selected location.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  The location filter is only visible on multi-location accounts. If you manage a single venue, all dashboard data already reflects your one location.
</Note>

## Exporting Dashboard Data

You can export a snapshot of your current dashboard view to CSV for offline analysis, reporting to stakeholders, or importing into spreadsheet tools.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Apply your desired filters">
    Set the date range and location before exporting so the CSV reflects exactly the data you need.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click Export">
    Select the **Export** button at the top-right of the dashboard panel.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose CSV format">
    In the export dialog, confirm **CSV** is selected and click **Download**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open the file">
    Your browser downloads a `.csv` file containing each widget's data as a separate section, labeled by widget name.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
  Review your dashboard every week at the same time — Monday morning works well for most FEC operators. Consistent weekly check-ins help you spot trends early, such as a dip in open rates or a surge in gift card sales around holidays, so you can act before a small shift becomes a big problem.
</Tip>
