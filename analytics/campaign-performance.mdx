Understanding how your campaigns perform is the fastest way to improve them. Rocket Effect gives you a dedicated performance report for every email and SMS campaign you run, showing you exactly how many guests engaged, what actions they took, and how much revenue you can attribute to each send. Use this data to double down on what works and fix what doesn't.

## Accessing a Campaign Report

<Steps>
  <Step title="Navigate to Campaigns">
    From the left sidebar, select **Marketing**, then click **Email Campaigns** or **SMS Campaigns** depending on the campaign type you want to review.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select a campaign">
    Find the campaign in your list and click its name to open the campaign detail view.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open the Performance tab">
    Click the **Performance** tab at the top of the campaign detail page to load the full analytics report.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Key Metrics Explained

Every campaign report surfaces the same core set of metrics. Use the table below to understand what each one measures and what a healthy range looks like for FECs.

| Metric | Definition | Benchmark Range (FECs) |
|---|---|---|
| **Sends** | Total number of messages dispatched to recipients | — (varies by list size) |
| **Deliveries** | Messages successfully delivered to a recipient's inbox or phone | > 95% of sends |
| **Open Rate** | Percentage of delivered emails that were opened by the recipient | 20–30% |
| **Click Rate** | Percentage of delivered messages where a recipient clicked at least one link | 3–8% |
| **Conversion Rate** | Percentage of recipients who completed the target action (card purchase, booking, coupon redemption) | 1–5% |
| **Revenue Attributed** | Total revenue generated by guests who converted through the campaign within the attribution window | — (goal-dependent) |

<Note>
  SMS campaigns do not have an open rate metric. For SMS, focus on click rate and conversion rate as your primary engagement indicators.
</Note>

## Comparing Campaigns

Rocket Effect lets you compare two or more campaigns side by side to identify what separates your best-performing sends from the rest.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Go to the Campaign list">
    Navigate to **Marketing → Email Campaigns** (or SMS Campaigns) to see all your campaigns.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select campaigns to compare">
    Check the box next to each campaign you want to include — you can select up to five at a time.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click Compare">
    Click the **Compare** button that appears in the action bar above the list.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review the comparison view">
    A side-by-side table appears showing each selected campaign's sends, open rate, click rate, conversion rate, and attributed revenue. Higher values are highlighted in green to make winners easy to spot.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Audience Insights

Beyond overall campaign numbers, Rocket Effect breaks performance down by the audience segments included in a campaign so you can see which guest groups responded best.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the campaign report">
    Follow the steps in [Accessing a Campaign Report](#accessing-a-campaign-report) to reach the Performance tab.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Scroll to Audience Breakdown">
    Below the top-level metrics, find the **Audience Breakdown** section. Each segment included in the campaign is listed with its own open rate, click rate, and conversion rate.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Identify high-performing segments">
    Look for segments with above-benchmark click and conversion rates. These groups are your most receptive audiences for similar offers in the future.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Using Data to Improve Future Campaigns

<Accordion title="Low open rate (below 20%)">
  A low open rate almost always points to a subject line or sender name problem. The email is being delivered but isn't compelling enough to open.

  - Rewrite your subject line to lead with a specific benefit or urgency ("Your $5 play credit expires Friday").
  - Avoid spam-trigger words like "FREE," all-caps text, or excessive punctuation.
  - Check your sender name — guests open emails from names they recognize, not generic system addresses.
  - Run an A/B test on your next campaign with two different subject lines to gather data before a full send.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Low click rate (below 3%)">
  If guests open the email but don't click, the message body or call-to-action needs work.

  - Make sure you have one clear, prominent call-to-action button above the fold.
  - Reduce the number of competing links — too many choices leads to no choice.
  - Ensure the offer in the subject line matches the content inside; mismatches break trust.
  - Check that links and buttons are rendering correctly on mobile devices, where most FEC guests read email.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Low conversion rate (below 1%)">
  Clicks are happening but guests aren't completing the desired action. The issue likely lives on the landing page or in the offer itself.

  - Review the page guests land on — is it fast-loading, mobile-friendly, and focused on one action?
  - Make the redemption process as short as possible (fewer form fields, guest checkout options).
  - Consider whether the offer is compelling enough relative to comparable promotions guests have seen before.
</Accordion>

<Tip>
  A/B test your subject lines on every major campaign send. In Rocket Effect, split your audience 50/50, let the test run for at least four hours, then promote the winning variant to the remaining recipients. Even a 5-point improvement in open rate compounds significantly across your annual campaign calendar.
</Tip>
