After creating your Rocket Effect account, a few minutes of configuration ensures that every guest-facing page, automated campaign, and booking confirmation reflects your brand accurately. This guide walks you through the core setup tasks — from your center profile to team roles and billing — so your account is production-ready from day one.

<Tip>
  Complete your center profile and connect at least one integration **before** publishing your online sales channels or launching marketing campaigns. Guest-facing pages pull your logo, brand colors, and contact details directly from your profile, and campaigns require a live data source to build accurate audience segments.
</Tip>

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Account Settings">
    Click your center name or avatar in the top-right corner of the Rocket Effect dashboard, then select **Account Settings** from the dropdown. This is your central hub for profile, team, notification, and billing management.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Configure your center profile and team">
    Use the tabs below to complete both sections before continuing.

    <Tabs>
      <Tab title="Center Profile">
        Navigate to **Account Settings → Profile** and fill in every field. Rocket Effect uses these details across storefront pages, booking confirmations, and email footers.

        | Field | Where it appears |
        |---|---|
        | **Center name** | Storefront header, email subject lines, booking pages |
        | **Address** | Booking confirmation emails, guest-facing venue info |
        | **Phone number** | Contact footer on emails and landing pages |
        | **Logo** | Storefront, emails, digital waivers, gift card designs |
        | **Brand colors** | Buttons, banners, and call-to-action elements sitewide |

        **To upload your logo:**
        1. Click **Upload Logo** and select a PNG or SVG file (minimum 200 × 200 px, transparent background recommended).
        2. Preview the result in the live example panel on the right.
        3. Click **Save Profile** when you're satisfied.

        **To set brand colors:**
        1. Click the color swatch next to **Primary Color** and enter your hex code or use the color picker.
        2. Repeat for **Accent Color** (used for buttons and highlights).
        3. Click **Save Profile**.
      </Tab>

      <Tab title="Team Management">
        Navigate to **Account Settings → Team** to invite staff members and assign them the right level of access.

        **Inviting a team member:**
        1. Click **Invite Member**.
        2. Enter the staff member's email address.
        3. Select a role from the dropdown (see role descriptions below).
        4. Click **Send Invitation** — they'll receive an email with a link to join your account.

        **Available roles:**

        | Role | Access level |
        |---|---|
        | **Admin** | Full access to all settings, billing, integrations, and data |
        | **Manager** | Access to campaigns, bookings, analytics, and online sales; cannot modify billing or integrations |
        | **Marketing Staff** | Access to campaigns, landing pages, and audience segments only |

        <Note>
          Invited team members must create their own Rocket Effect login using the email address you specified. Each member logs in independently and actions are attributed to their individual account in the audit log.
        </Note>

        **Removing or changing a team member's role:**
        1. Find the member in the **Team** list.
        2. Click the **⋯** menu next to their name.
        3. Select **Edit Role** or **Remove Member**.
      </Tab>
    </Tabs>
  </Step>

  <Step title="Set notification preferences">
    Navigate to **Account Settings → Notifications** to control which alerts Rocket Effect sends to your email address.

    Choose from the following notification categories:

    - **Booking alerts** — receive an email each time a party or event booking is submitted, modified, or cancelled.
    - **Campaign reports** — get a summary email after each campaign send, including open rates, clicks, and conversions.
    - **Weekly performance digest** — a rolled-up snapshot of revenue, new guests, and top campaigns delivered every Monday.
    - **Integration sync errors** — immediate alerts if Rocket Effect loses its connection to your POS or card system.

    Toggle each category on or off and click **Save Notification Preferences**.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Manage billing and your plan">
    Navigate to **Account Settings → Billing** to review your current plan, upgrade, or update your payment method.

    - **View your plan** — see which features are included and your current usage against any limits.
    - **Upgrade your plan** — click **Change Plan** to compare tiers and unlock additional contacts, integrations, or campaign sends.
    - **Update payment method** — click **Edit Payment** to add or replace a credit card on file.
    - **Download invoices** — click any invoice in the billing history table to download a PDF receipt.

    <Warning>
      Downgrading your plan may disable active features such as SMS campaigns or advanced analytics. Review the feature comparison carefully before confirming a plan change.
    </Warning>
  </Step>

  <Step title="Secure your account">
    Navigate to **Account Settings → Security** to update your password and review account access.

    **Reset your password:**
    1. Click **Change Password**.
    2. Enter your current password, then your new password twice.
    3. Click **Update Password** — you'll receive a confirmation email.

    **Forgot your password?** Click **Sign In** at [rocketeffect.app](https://rocketeffect.app), then select **Forgot password?** and follow the link sent to your email.

    **Review active sessions:** Under **Active Sessions**, you can see all devices currently logged into your account. Click **Revoke** next to any session you don't recognize to sign that device out immediately.
  </Step>
</Steps>
