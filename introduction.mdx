## How It Works

<Steps>
  <Step title="Connect Your Systems">
    Link Rocket Effect to your existing card system or POS — Intercard, Embed, Sacoa, Brunswick, Clubspeed, or RTS. Your guest data and transaction history flow in automatically.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Build Your Guest Profiles">
    Rocket Effect builds unified guest profiles from your integrated data, capturing game play history, spending patterns, and contact information for targeted marketing.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Run Campaigns & Sell Online">
    Create automated email and SMS campaigns, launch online card and gift card sales, and set up party booking — all from your Rocket Effect dashboard.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Measure & Optimize">
    Track campaign performance, conversion rates, and revenue on your personalized analytics dashboard to continuously improve your results.
  </Step>
</Steps>
