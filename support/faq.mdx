Whether you're just getting started or looking to get more out of the platform, this page covers the most common questions from Rocket Effect users. Browse by topic below, or reach out to the support team if you need additional help.

## Getting Started

<AccordionGroup title="Getting Started">
  <Accordion title="Is Rocket Effect free to get started?">
    Rocket Effect offers a free onboarding consultation so you can see the platform in action before committing. Pricing is based on your center's needs — contact the Rocket Effect team at [support@rocketeffect.com](mailto:support@rocketeffect.com) for a quote tailored to your FEC.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="What software do I need before signing up?">
    Rocket Effect is a cloud-based platform, so all you need is a modern web browser and an internet connection. To unlock the full feature set — including guest data sync, card balance lookups, and loyalty tracking — you'll also need an active account with one of the supported card systems: Intercard, Embed, Sacoa, Brunswick (Meriq), Clubspeed, or RTS.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="How long does it take to get set up?">
    Most centers are fully live within **one to two weeks**. Your onboarding includes connecting your card system integration, configuring your online sales widgets, and setting up your first automated marketing campaigns. The Rocket Effect team guides you through every step.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## Integrations

<AccordionGroup title="Integrations">
  <Accordion title="Which card systems does Rocket Effect connect with?">
    Rocket Effect natively integrates with the six most widely used FEC card and booking platforms:

    - **Intercard**
    - **Embed**
    - **Sacoa**
    - **Brunswick (Meriq)**
    - **Clubspeed**
    - **RTS**

    See the [Integrations Overview](/concepts/integrations-overview) page for details on what data each integration provides.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="My card system isn't on the list — can I still use Rocket Effect?">
    Some features — such as automated online sales and real-time card balance sync — require a direct integration. However, you can still use Rocket Effect's marketing, booking, and analytics tools independently. Contact [support@rocketeffect.com](mailto:support@rocketeffect.com) to discuss your setup and explore whether a custom connection is feasible.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Does the integration work if I have multiple locations?">
    Yes. Rocket Effect supports multi-location FECs. Each location connects to its own card system account, and you can manage campaigns, reports, and guest data either per-location or across all locations from a single dashboard. Reach out to the support team to configure a multi-location setup.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## Online Sales

<AccordionGroup title="Online Sales">
  <Accordion title="Can guests purchase play cards from my website?">
    Yes. Rocket Effect provides an embeddable widget that lets guests buy new play cards directly from your website. Once payment is confirmed, the card value is loaded into your card system automatically. Visit the [Play Cards](/online-sales/play-cards) page for setup instructions.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="How does card reloading work online?">
    Guests who already have a play card can reload it online using the same purchase widget. They enter their card number or log in with their guest profile, choose a reload amount, and complete checkout. The balance is credited to their card in real time through the integration with your card system.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Do I need a separate payment processor?">
    Rocket Effect connects to your existing payment processor rather than replacing it. During setup, you'll configure your payment credentials so that all transactions flow through your preferred processor. If you don't yet have a processor, the onboarding team can recommend compatible options.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## Marketing

<AccordionGroup title="Marketing">
  <Accordion title="How do guests opt in to SMS messages?">
    Guests opt in to SMS marketing at the point of capture — for example, when they create a guest profile online, complete a digital waiver, or sign up through one of your landing pages or pop-ups. Rocket Effect automatically records consent and only sends SMS messages to guests who have explicitly opted in, keeping you compliant with messaging regulations.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Can I send campaigns to just a specific audience segment?">
    Yes. Before sending any campaign, you select an audience segment based on criteria such as visit frequency, last visit date, birthday month, loyalty tier, or total spend. This means you can target lapsed guests with a win-back offer, reward your most loyal customers, or wish birthday guests a happy birthday — all automatically. Learn more on the [Email Campaigns](/marketing/email-campaigns) and [SMS Campaigns](/marketing/sms-campaigns) pages.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="What types of automated campaigns can I set up?">
    Rocket Effect supports a wide range of automation triggers, including:

    - **Welcome series** — sent when a guest creates a profile
    - **Birthday campaigns** — email or SMS sent before a guest's birthday
    - **Win-back campaigns** — triggered after a guest hasn't visited in a set number of days
    - **Loyalty milestones** — messages sent when a guest reaches a points threshold or earns a new VIP tier
    - **Post-visit follow-ups** — sent after a guest checks out

    Visit [Concepts: Campaigns](/concepts/campaigns) for a full breakdown of automation types.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## Booking

<AccordionGroup title="Booking">
  <Accordion title="Can guests book parties and events online?">
    Yes. Rocket Effect includes an online party and event booking flow that guests can access directly from your website. You define your available packages, time slots, and capacity, and guests self-serve from start to finish. All bookings appear in your [Manage Bookings](/booking/managing-bookings) dashboard for your team to action.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="How do digital waivers work?">
    When a guest completes a booking — or arrives at your center — Rocket Effect can prompt them to sign a digital waiver. The signed waiver is stored against their guest profile, so you always have a record on file. You can customize waiver content, require re-signing after a set period, and download signed waivers at any time. See the [Digital Waivers](/booking/digital-waivers) page for full details.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## Analytics

<AccordionGroup title="Analytics">
  <Accordion title="How often does dashboard data update?">
    Your Rocket Effect dashboard refreshes **automatically every 24 hours**. Key metrics — including campaign open rates, click-throughs, and online sales revenue — are updated on a daily basis. Some integration-dependent data, such as card balance and gameplay history, syncs according to your card system's export schedule. Visit the [Analytics Dashboard](/analytics/dashboard) page to learn how to read your key metrics.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Can I export my data?">
    Yes. From the Analytics dashboard you can export reports as CSV files for use in spreadsheet tools or for sharing with your team. Available exports include campaign performance summaries, guest profile lists, and gameplay history. Navigate to the relevant report and look for the **Export** button in the top-right corner of the table or chart.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
