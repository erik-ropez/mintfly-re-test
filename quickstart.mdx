Rocket Effect gets your Family Entertainment Center up and running with online sales, automated marketing, and party bookings faster than any other platform. Follow the steps below to go from sign-up to your first live campaign — no technical experience required.

<Note>
  Rocket Effect offers a free plan that requires no credit card to get started. You can explore the platform, connect an integration, and set up online sales before committing to a paid tier.
</Note>

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create your account">
    Head to [rocketeffect.app/register](https://rocketeffect.app/register) and sign up using your **email address**, **Google account**, or **Facebook account**. After verifying your email, you'll land on the Rocket Effect dashboard and be prompted to complete your center profile.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Complete your center profile">
    Fill in the essential details that power your storefront, booking pages, and marketing communications:

    - **Center name** — appears on guest-facing pages and emails
    - **Location** — street address used for local SEO and booking confirmations
    - **Time zone** — ensures campaigns, reports, and booking slots display in your local time

    Save your profile before moving to the next step. You can add your logo and brand colors later in **Account Settings → Profile**.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Connect your first integration">
    Rocket Effect syncs guest data, play card balances, and transaction history directly from your point-of-sale or card system. Navigate to **Settings → Integrations** and choose the system your center already uses:

    | Integration | Best for |
    |---|---|
    | **Intercard** | Debit card & cashless play systems |
    | **Embed** | All-in-one FEC management platforms |
    | **Sacoa** | Cashless debit card systems |
    | **Brunswick (Meriq)** | Bowling and multi-attraction venues |
    | **Clubspeed** | Go-kart and multi-activity centers |
    | **RTS** | Redemption and ticketing systems |

    Enter your credentials when prompted and click **Test Connection** to confirm the link is live. Once connected, Rocket Effect begins importing your guest profiles automatically.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Set up your first online sales channel">
    With your integration active, you're ready to sell. Go to **Online Sales** in the left navigation and choose your starting point:

    - **Play Cards** — sell rechargeable play card credits directly from your website or a Rocket Effect-hosted storefront page.
    - **Gift Cards** — offer branded digital gift cards guests can purchase and redeem in-venue or online.

    Configure your denominations, set pricing, and enable the channel. Rocket Effect generates a shareable link and embeddable widget you can add to your website right away.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Launch your first marketing campaign">
    Navigate to **Marketing → Email Campaigns** or **Marketing → SMS Campaigns** and click **New Campaign**. Rocket Effect provides ready-made templates for common FEC goals — birthday outreach, lapsed-guest win-backs, and seasonal promotions.

    1. Select a template or start from scratch.
    2. Choose your audience segment (e.g., all guests, guests inactive for 60 days, upcoming birthdays).
    3. Personalize your subject line and message body.
    4. Schedule the send or set it to trigger automatically based on guest behavior.
    5. Click **Launch Campaign** — Rocket Effect handles the rest.
  </Step>
</Steps>
